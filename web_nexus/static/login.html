TERMS & CONDITIONS

Who runs this
Nexus is a personal project built by Xavier Scott (THYFWX). It is not a product. There is no company. It runs on Cloudflare's edge network with AI APIs from Groq, Google, Hugging Face, and Replicate.

What gets logged
Every prompt you send and every reply the AI returns is logged for debugging and quality work. Your IP, browser, OS, locale, and timezone are recorded per request. Logs are private, never sold, never shared with third parties, but they do exist on the server.

What does NOT get stored about a guest
Guest sessions are anonymous. No account is created. No persistent memory of past conversations. The instant you close this tab, your session is gone. If you want the AI to remember you across sessions, sign in with Google.

Conversation history during your session
The terminal keeps the last few messages in your browser's memory only. Enough for the AI to follow context within one conversation. Cleared on tab close.

What you CAN do as a guest
Three AI chat modes are open to guests: Nexus (general), Coder (programming), and Education (structured learning). Guests have a daily message limit; signing in with Google raises it.

Image generation requires Google sign-in
To generate images (wallpapers, concept art, illustrations), you must be signed into a Google account. Google users get 15 images/day; supporters who donate get 150/day. Guests are blocked from image gen entirely.

All image gen is SFW
Image generation produces clean, safe-for-work output across every mode. No explicit / NSFW image generation exists anywhere on Nexus.

Unfiltered mode (sign-in required)
Unfiltered is a candid chat mode that hedges less on mature topics. It requires a signed-in Google account and a one-time 18+ confirmation. Guests cannot access it.

Acceptable use
Don't use Nexus to harass real people, dox someone, attack systems, generate content sexualizing minors, or do anything illegal in your jurisdiction. Abusive sessions get terminated and the IP blocked. Critical content patterns (CSAM, weapon synthesis, body disposal, mass violence) auto-lock the user and alert the operator.
